Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers for our religious and holiday tour packages.

Can we customize dates and combine multiple holy sites (e.g. Makkah + Egypt)?

Yes! Our Ziyarat specialists customize multi-destination itineraries. You can combine Umrah (Makkah & Madinah) with historic tours of Cairo/Alexandria, the shrines of Karbala & Najaf, or Jordan's historical landmarks.

What is included in the Indian and International holiday packages?

All packages include verified 3, 4, or 5-star hotels, daily breakfasts, sanitized private/luxury coach transport, sightseeing entry tickets, and full support from our on-ground tour coordinator.

What are the differences between Normal, Premium, and Pro VIP tiers?

Normal: Value-focused 3-Star comfort with shared AC coaches and basic tours. Premium: 4-Star/Near-center stays with full buffet meals and small-group tours. Pro VIP: 5-Star front-row suites, dedicated luxury private vehicles (GMC/Sedan), and personalized 24/7 private butlers.
